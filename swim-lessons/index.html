Swim Lessons

Swim lessons provide an amazing opportunity to learn to swim in a fun and safe environment. Our lessons are taught by qualified swim instructors who focus on teaching kids new skills and most importantly, safety awareness. Private lessons are taught year-round while groups are offered from June to August.

Contact Michael Avina at swimlessons@cogganaquatics.org for more information.

Private Lessons (Age 3 – Adult)

Private lessons are individualized swimming instruction with a qualified staff instructor. They are taught year round on a first come first serve basis. Private lessons are 25 minutes long, focusing on specific problem areas or concerns. We offer private lessons 7 days a week during normal pool operation hours.

Semi-Private Lessons (Age 3 – Adult) offer one-on-two instruction for swimmers similar in age and swimming ability. With the same format as private lessons, just twice the fun.

Levels

Guppy: Water introduction for young children

Focus: The object of this class is to help students feel comfortable in the water. Students will work on bubble blowing, breath control, putting their head in the water, floating, kicking, retrieving submerged rings and introducing arms skills.

Seahorse: Fundamental skills building class

Requirement: Students must be able to float on front and back comfortably without support for 10 seconds. Be able to comfortably submerge head underwater.

Focus: Students will begin front/back floating, rolling on their back to breathe, getting back to the wall with assistance, and glides and diving for submerged rings.

Sea Turtle: Focus on unsupported crawl skills (freestyle/backstroke)

Requirement: Students will focus on mastering rolling on their back to breathe, coordinating freestyle arm strokes and gliding independently.

Focus: Students will focus on indepentent front/back floats, rolling on their back to breathe while coordinating arm circles on their tummy and kicks.

Stingray: Stroke Development of freestyle with side breathing

Requirement: Students must be able to roll on their back to breathe and be capable of jumping from the side of the pool and swimming 5 yards.

Focus: Students will learn to do freestyle with side breathing, backstroke, dolphin kick and breathstroke kick.

Seal: Increase endurance of freestyle and backstroke

Requirement: Students must be able to swim 10 yards of freestyle, backstroke, and dolphin kick.

Focus: Students will continue to build their freestyle and backstroke, and breathe and will be introduced Breaststroke will be introduced to kneeling front dives as well as breaststroke pull and timing.

Dolphin: Refinement & coordination of key strokes

Requirement: Students must be able to swim freestyle and backstroke 15–25 yards with side breathing, dolphin kick and breaststroke kick.

Students will continue to refine all 4 strokes and standing dives while building endurance. While also learning flip turns and sculling.

Orca: Polish key strokes and more endurance

Requirement: Students must be able to swim at least 25 yards of freestyle, 25 yards backstroke and understand the basics of breaststroke and butterfly.

Focus: Students will be introduced to open turns, flip turns and shallow dives, as well as endurance building for all strokes. Stroke refinement is the key in this class. This class is designed as a prep class for Wind N’ Sea novice swim team.

Policies

Due to the nature of the group classes, no sign-ups will be allowed after the first day of the session. Classes may be cancelled due to staff and pool constraints. Minimum of 3 kids per class. There are no make-up lessons or refunds once a session has started. Lessons are held rain or shine.

Refunds

All refund requests must be submitted in writing to the Staff and Programs Director. Refund request forms are available at the Coggan Family Aquatic Complex Front Desk. Refunds requested prior to one week from the start of the class will receive a full refund minus a $10 service charge per class. Refunds requested within one week prior to the start of the session will receive a 75% refund. Refunds requested after the start of the session will receive a 50% refund. No refund will be given after the first week of the class. No refunds or make-ups for missed classes. No credit or refunds will be given due to inclement weather. Students absent 3 or more days due to illness/injury within one session, with a doctors note, may request a prorated credit or refund. If the Coggan Family Aquatic Complex cancels a class, a credit will be issued towards any Coggan Family Aquatic Complex class or program.

Returned Checks

For any returned check, a $35 charge will be assessed.
